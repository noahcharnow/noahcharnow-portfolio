The work

The Visitor Center opened in New York with a Google installation inside. My job was the billboard outside, announcing the opening, matched to the installation so the two read as one thing.

I led the design and pushed it through our in-house production team to get the artwork print ready against the opening date. Then it went up.

Made presidential work, from a billboard.
